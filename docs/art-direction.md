# SUPERGROOVE 2027

Creative direction: “Ein Rhythmus. Viele Herzen.”

Brazilian modernist colour and festival typography meet a warm community story. Deep forest green, lime yellow, coral and pink provide contrast. Soft rotation, rhythm bars and a moving ribbon create energy; all can be paused and reduced-motion preferences are respected.

## Illustration brief

Website asset: `dist/assets/brasil-2027.webp`.

A joyful samba gathering with community and generosity at its heart. Landscape illustration, no text. Contemporary Brazilian modernist illustration, hand-painted gouache and cut-paper shapes, subtle grain, luscious botanical forms, forest green, chartreuse yellow, coral, turquoise and cream. An imaginary Brazilian coastal landscape inspired by Rio, with layered emerald mountains, a Sugarloaf-shaped peak, sparkling bay and a warm sun. In the foreground, diverse adults make music on surdo drums and tambourines and dance in festive summer clothing. Tropical leaves, banana palms and heliconia blooms frame the scene, with a toucan on a branch. Welcoming, soulful and exuberant. No lettering, logos, flags or border.

The artwork is delivered as WebP and presented as illustration, not documentation of the future event.

Event facts are limited to the organizer's announcement: 3 April 2027, Ronald Reloaded, Festa de Samba and a workshop with Toby „Tamandua“ Russell from Badauê Paris. Venue, times, registration and further line-up remain pending.
